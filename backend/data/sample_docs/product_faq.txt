Acme Home Hub — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Acme Home Hub?
The Acme Home Hub is a smart-home controller that connects to Wi-Fi and
Zigbee devices, letting you control lights, thermostats, and locks from one
app. It launched in March 2026.

How many devices can it support?
A single Home Hub supports up to 150 connected devices. If you need more,
Acme recommends pairing two hubs using the "Hub Mesh" feature introduced in
firmware version 2.3.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Local automations (routines that don't call external services, like
"turn on the porch light at sunset") continue to run during an internet
outage. Voice assistant integration and remote access require an internet
connection.

What is the battery backup time?
The Home Hub includes a built-in battery that keeps the device running for
up to 4 hours during a power outage, preserving local automations.

How do I reset it to factory settings?
Hold the reset button on the back of the unit for 12 seconds until the LED
ring flashes red three times, then release. This erases all paired devices
and routines.
