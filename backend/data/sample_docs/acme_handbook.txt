Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (Internal, v3)

Working Hours
Acme Robotics operates on flexible hours. Employees are expected to be online
for core collaboration hours between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time, Monday
through Friday. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their own
schedule with their manager's agreement.

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year,
credited monthly at a rate of roughly 1.83 days per month. Unused PTO can be
carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year; any
balance above that is forfeited on December 31st. New hires begin accruing
PTO from their start date and may use accrued PTO after their first 30 days.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval.
Fully remote arrangements (5 days per week) require written sign-off from
both the employee's manager and the People Operations team, renewed annually.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 can be submitted directly through the Expensify
app and are auto-approved. Expenses of $75 or more require manager approval
before submission. All expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the
purchase date; late submissions are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by
Finance.

Equipment
New employees receive a laptop, a monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office
setup stipend, redeemable within the first 60 days of employment.
